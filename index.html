SAP Solutions

The proposed solution architecture

Three integrated SAP products covering the full cycle from local close to group consolidation.

🗓️
SAP Advanced Financial Closing

Orchestration and automation of the local close. Defines and schedules a global close plan across all entities, automates tasks with dependencies, workflows and notifications, and provides real-time status visibility with full audit trail.

Orchestration Automation Compliance Real-time monitoring
📊
SAP S/4HANA Group Reporting

IFRS consolidation layer replacing spreadsheets. Collects data from SAP and non-SAP ERPs, performs consolidation with intercompany eliminations, and generates financial statements, cash flow and P&L with integrated dashboarding.

IFRS consolidation Multi-ERP Intercompany Dashboarding
✅
SAP BlackLine

Accounting automation and reconciliations. Handles account reconciliation, automatic substantiation and embedded SOX/ICFR controls. Significantly reduces the manual workload of the finance team.

Key Takeaways

The key messages of the presentation

Six core concepts to evaluate the SAP opportunity for Alfasigma.

1
Current complexity is a brake on growth
With 17 ERP systems across 30+ jurisdictions, Alfasigma faces structural friction — not a resource problem. Manual processes and spreadsheets expose the company to compliance risk and slow down strategic decisions.
2
The window to act is now, not tomorrow
The RISE with SAP migration already underway and the recent acquisitions (Intercept, Nordic Group) create a one-time window. Acting now means capitalising on the ongoing transformation; waiting means adding complexity on top of complexity.
3
SAP offers an end-to-end platform, not isolated products
SAP AFC + Group Reporting + BlackLine cover the full cycle from local close to IFRS group consolidation. A single platform eliminates custom integrations, reduces maintenance costs and guarantees data consistency.
4
The benefits are quantified and benchmarked
The closing cycle halves (from 10 to 5 days), with an estimated bottom-line benefit of up to €0.5M annually for €1.7B in revenue. The Pfizer case — from 8 to 6 days to close and reduced manual work — proves these are real results, not promises.
5
AI is not the future: it is available today
SAP Autonomous Finance brings agentic intelligence to the financial close: automated journal entries, intelligent intercompany reconciliation, real-time anomaly detection. The Financial Closing Assistant is on the roadmap for Q3/2026.
6
Value goes beyond Go-Live: it is a continuous journey
The roadmap includes executive workshops, quantified business cases, data assessments and an adoption journey. Value is built after Go-Live, not only during implementation. Alfasigma can start with a phased, measurable approach.
